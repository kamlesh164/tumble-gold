About The Tumble GOLD

The Tumble Gold is a one-of-a-kind wellness and mobility program designed exclusively for adults aged 50 and above.

Our mission is to help seniors regain strength, balance, and flexibility through movement — so they can live active, joyful, and independent lives.

We combine fitness science, physiotherapy support, and a friendly environment to make aging a confident, happy experience.

Have Questions? Here Are Quick Answers
To Some Of The Most Common Queries

Everything you need to know about Tumble GOLD
Is Tumble GOLD suitable for completely beginners?
Yes! Our sessions are designed for all abilities. We start with a gentle assessment and adapt every movement for your comfort. No prior experience required!
What if I have existing health conditions or injuries?
Please inform your trainer about any medical issue, injury, or limitation before joining. Our coaches can modify exercises and offer extra guidance for maximum safety.
What should I wear and bring to sessions?
Wear comfortable, breathable clothing and supportive shoes. We recommend carrying a water bottle and a small towel.
How long are the sessions?
Each group session lasts about 45-50 minutes, including warm-up, main exercises, and cool-down.
Can I try a session before committing to a membership?
Absolutely—your first trial session is free! Meet our team, join a group, and experience the difference risk-free.
What makes Tumble GOLD different from regular gyms?
We specialize in 50+ fitness. Our trainers are experts in gentle, functional movement. Sessions are small, friendly, and always adapted for your needs.
Are there any age restrictions?
All adults above 50 are welcome! Our groups are tailored to suit fitness levels from complete beginner to active seniors.
Can I bring a friend or spouse?
Yes! Training with friends or family can boost motivation and makes sessions more enjoyable. Please let us know in advance for trial scheduling.
